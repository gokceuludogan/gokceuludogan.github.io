<span class="anchor" id="about"></span>

<p class="eyebrow">COMPUTATIONAL BIOLOGY · MACHINE LEARNING · NLP</p>

I am a PhD candidate in Computer Engineering at **Boğaziçi University**, advised by [Prof. Arzucan Özgür](https://www.cmpe.boun.edu.tr/~arzucan/) and Elif Ozkirimli. My research develops computational methods that connect protein sequence, structure, function.

My work spans protein language models, graph neural networks, ontology-aware learning. I also work in cheminformatics and low-resource natural language processing, with an emphasis on useful datasets, rigorous benchmarks, and reproducible research software.
